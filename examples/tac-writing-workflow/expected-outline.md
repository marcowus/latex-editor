# Expected structured outline

## P0: Main thesis to verify

For the specified simulated nonlinear process and stated operating region, the proposed controller may improve a defined tracking or constraint metric relative to named baselines, subject to evidence that remains to be produced.

## Argument dependencies

| Dependency | Evidence required before claiming it |
| --- | --- |
| C1: Problem scope | Plant equations, measurements, constraints, initial-condition set, and horizon |
| C2: Method mechanism | Exact controller and predictor definitions, training data, and online information flow |
| C3: Theoretical property | Assumptions, theorem statement, proof, and conclusion domain |
| C4: Empirical result | Frozen protocol, baselines, seeds, metrics, and adverse cases |

## Boundaries

- A simulation result is not an industrial deployment claim.
- Better one-step prediction does not by itself prove safer closed-loop control.
- No stability claim should be made until the stated theorem assumptions and proof are complete.
